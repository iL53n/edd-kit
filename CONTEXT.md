# EDD Kit Domain Language

## Requirement

A stable, human-readable business rule that acceptance must satisfy.

## Case

A concrete application scenario used to evaluate one or more requirements.

## Control

A labelled worked example used to check whether a grader accepts or rejects a known outcome.

## Domain approval

A human decision that the requirements and cases represent the intended business behavior.

## Technical validation

A human decision that the graders and controls meaningfully measure the approved expectations.

## Audit

A mechanical comparison of grader decisions with the declared control labels. A passing audit is
limited evidence about those controls, not proof that the grader is universally reliable.

## Execution evidence

Recorded observations from evaluating a specific application target under a declared configuration.

## Acceptance

A PASS decision supported by current domain approval, technical validation, grader audit, and
candidate execution evidence for the same criteria and target identities.
